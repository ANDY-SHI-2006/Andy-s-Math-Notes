Exercise 5. Let P an be a conditionally convergent series of real numbers, and let S be a given real
number. Prove that there is a rearrangement P bn of P an which converges to the sum S.
Hint: exercise 4. Remark: note that this result not only tells us that rearrangement of a conditionally
convergent series may alter its sum, but also tells us that we can rearrange the terms of a conditionally
convergent series to make it converge to any given real number! This result is often referred to as the
Riemann’s rearrangement theorem.
